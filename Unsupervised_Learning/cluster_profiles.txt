INTERPRETATION

K-Means (main segmentation)
- High-Volume Buyers (37.8%): buy often and spend the most. Churn rate 44.0%. Protect them with loyalty rewards.
- Low-Engagement Customers (31.5%): few emails opened, many abandoned carts. Churn rate 54.1%, the highest. First priority for re-engagement.
- Engaged Light Buyers (30.7%): buy little but respond to emails. Churn rate 40.9%, the lowest. Offer recommendations to raise order size.

GMM (supporting segmentation)
- Four purchase tiers: One-time, Occasional, Regular and Heavy Users. Churn rates are 48.6%, 47.8%, 46.8% and 42.6%.
- Membership probabilities flag customers whose tier is uncertain (1.08% have confidence below 80%).

Overall: the overall churn rate is 46.2%. Neither algorithm finds sharply separated groups, so segments are used as context for the Phase 1 churn predictions, not as predictions themselves.
