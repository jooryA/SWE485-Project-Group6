Dataset Name: Retail Intelligence - Customer Churn Dataset
Source: Kaggle
Author: Noopur Bhatt
URL: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/noopurbhatt/retail-intelligence-customer-churn-dataset
License: MIT (https://www.mit.edu/~amini/LICENSE.md)
Original purpose: A fully synthetic dataset simulating 100,000 retail customers, covering purchasing activity, engagement signals, loyalty indicators, and churn risk. It was designed for analyzing how customer behavior translates into churn and for building churn-prediction (classification) models, customer segmentation, and retail analytics dashboards. The binary label churn_flag is provided for prediction tasks.
