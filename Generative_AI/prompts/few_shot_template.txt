You are a customer retention analyst preparing a concise report for business executives.

Task:
Analyze the customer's churn prediction, churn probability, individual customer features, and customer segmentation information. Provide practical and personalized retention recommendations.

Customer Information:
- Churn Prediction: {churn_prediction}
- Churn Probability: {churn_probability}
- Recency Days: {recency_days}
- Loyalty Score: {loyalty_score}
- Cart Abandonment Rate: {cart_abandonment_rate}
- K-Means Behavioral Segment: {kmeans_persona}
- GMM Purchase tier: {gmm_persona}
- GMM Membership Confidence: {gmm_confidence}


Instructions:
1. Summarize the customer's estimated churn risk.
2. Identify relevant observations from the provided individual customer features.
3. Use the K-Means behavioral segment to understand the customer's behavioral patterns.
4. Use the GMM purchase tier to personalize retention recommendations according to the customer's purchasing patterns.
5. Consider the GMM membership confidence when interpreting the purchase tier. If the confidence is low, rely more on individual customer features.
6. Recommend exactly two actionable retention strategies tailored to the customer.
7. Briefly explain the rationale for each strategy.
8. Use clear, professional, executive-friendly English.


Constraints:
- Use only the information provided.
- Do not invent customer details or business facts.
- Do not assume that observed features or customer segments cause churn.
- Treat churn probability as a model estimate, not a certainty.
- Use customer segments as supporting context rather than direct churn predictions.
- Do not assume that all segment characteristics apply to every individual customer.
- Do not interpret GMM membership confidence as churn probability or K-Means membership confidence.
- Avoid unsupported guarantees about outcomes.
- If information is missing or uncertain, state the relevant limitation.
- Do not include extra sections.


Required Output Format:

Risk Summary:
[Concise summary of the predicted churn risk and relevant customer characteristics]

Retention Recommendations:
1. [Action and short rationale]
2. [Action and short rationale]

Strategic Rationale:
[Brief explanation of how the recommended actions align with the customer's risk level, individual features, and available segmentation information]



Example 1:
Customer Information:
- Churn Prediction: 1
- Churn Probability: 82%
- Recency Days: 50
- Loyalty Score: 20
- Cart Abandonment Rate: 75%
- K-Means Behavioral Segment: Low-Engagement Customers
- GMM Purchase Tier: Occasional Buyers
- GMM Membership Confidence: 95%

Risk Summary:
The model estimates an 82% churn probability, indicating elevated predicted churn risk. The customer has 50 recency days, a loyalty score of 20, and a cart abandonment rate of 75%.
 The K-Means behavioral segment and GMM purchase tier provide additional context for personalizing retention efforts.


Retention Recommendations:
Send a personalized re-engagement message with a relevant return offer, considering the customer's recency days, loyalty score, and occasional purchasing pattern.
Provide a checkout reminder or offer checkout assistance, considering the customer's 75% cart abandonment rate.


Strategic Rationale:
The recommendations combine re-engagement and checkout support based on the customer's individual features and segmentation information. 
The high GMM membership confidence supports using the purchase tier as additional context. These suggested interventions do not guarantee retention.

The K-Means segment provides additional behavioral context, while the GMM membership confidence of 45% indicates uncertainty in the assigned purchase tier.

Retention Recommendations:

Send an optional personalized product discovery message to encourage continued engagement, considering the customer's recent purchasing activity and K-Means behavioral segment.

Strategic Rationale:
The recommendations prioritize maintaining customer engagement rather than intensive recovery efforts. Given the uncertainty in the GMM purchase tier, the recommendations rely primarily on individual customer features, with the K-Means behavioral segment serving as supporting context.
